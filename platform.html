How we move
a site to close.

OVMG doesn't build; we connect. We originate sites, assemble the right relationships, and orchestrate every party a data center transaction requires from first conversation to close.

§01 · Our Disciplines
01

Site Origination

We source and hold a curated portfolio of data center opportunities: greenfield, brownfield, and NTP-stage sites across multiple regions. Every site in our portfolio has been evaluated for power viability, location, and deal readiness before we bring it to a partner.

Greenfield · Brownfield · NTP→
02

Capital Introduction

We connect institutional capital, family offices, and private equity to data center opportunities at the right moment in the deal cycle. Our relationships span the full capital stack: equity, debt, and structured instruments, and we know which partners move at the pace each opportunity requires.

Equity · Debt · Structured Capital→
03

Offtaker & Operator Matching

We identify the hyperscalers, colocation operators, and enterprise tenants whose demand profile fits each site and we make the introduction count. The right offtaker at the right site changes everything about a deal's trajectory.

Hyperscale · Colocation · Enterprise→
04

Deal Process & Data Rooms

We build the decks, organize the data rooms, and manage every step of the transaction with the discipline a capital partner expects. Every opportunity we bring to market is documented, organized, and presented with clarity, from the first teaser to the final close.

Decks · Data Rooms · Transaction Management→
05

Partner Network

Through deep, ongoing relationships with energy, engineering, construction, and storage partners, we bring every technical capability a data center requires without the overhead of building it in-house. Our partners don't just support our deals; they help make them possible.

§02 · Our Standard

Built with the land
and community
in mind.

The founders of OVMG carry real backgrounds in environmental conservation and community development. These aren't credentials we bolt on; they are the lens through which we evaluate every site and every deal. As our portfolio grows, we are committed to integrating conservation practices, community benefit, and responsible land stewardship from day one.

01
Land & Habitat Preservation

We evaluate every site with an eye toward minimizing ecological disruption. Where development and conservation can coexist, we build toward that outcome, because land is not a commodity; it is a responsibility.

02
Community Benefit

Every data center we help bring to life has the potential to transform a community through jobs, infrastructure, and long-term investment. We build relationships with local stakeholders from the first site visit, not the last.

03
Responsible Resource Use

Data centers are resource-intensive by nature. We hold our sites and partner network to a standard that accounts for long-term water, land, and energy impact, and we make that part of the conversation at the deal stage, not after.

04
Conservation-First Site Selection

Our founders' history in environmental work shapes how we screen and underwrite opportunities. Sites that create irrecoverable ecological harm don't make it into our portfolio.
